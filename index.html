About

Dreadful Science is a series of short animated episodes. Each one takes a gross, strange or frightening claim, lets the Weird Professor run with it, then corrects him with a sourced answer. Sources are listed in every video description.

Episodes are made with AI. The characters are fiction. The facts are checked.
