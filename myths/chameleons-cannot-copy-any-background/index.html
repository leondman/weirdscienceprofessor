Chameleons cannot copy any background.

The Professor says

A chameleon can change colour to match any background or pattern.

Fact Check

Each chameleon species has a limited range of colours and patterns. Small shifts can help some blend in, but no chameleon can copy a chessboard or wallpaper. The bigger colour displays are mostly communication - rivals, mates, mood, temperature.
