Your stomach can dissolve a razor blade

The Professor says

Your stomach acid can dissolve a razor blade.

Fact Check

In a laboratory study, simulated gastric juice corroded razor blades over time. After 24 hours the blades weighed 63% of their original weight. This does not make swallowing metal safe: sharp objects can cause serious injury. Never try it. A lab corrosion result is not a safe way to remove a swallowed object.

Sources

  • Li et al., 1997: In vitro effects of simulated gastric juice on swallowed metal objects (PubMed)
