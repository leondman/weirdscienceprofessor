Swallowed gum does NOT stay for seven years.

The Professor says

Swallowed gum stays in your stomach for seven years.

Fact Check

It does not. Your body cannot digest chewing gum, but swallowed gum moves relatively intact through your digestive system and passes out in your stool. Very rarely, large amounts of swallowed gum combined with constipation have blocked intestines in children. Do not make swallowing gum a habit.

Sources

  • Mayo Clinic: Swallowing gum: Is it harmful?
