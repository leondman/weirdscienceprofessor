Bats are NOT blind. The 'blind as a bat' myth is wrong.

The Professor says

Bats are blind.

Fact Check

They are not. All bats have working eyes and can see. Vision varies by species, and some bats see better than people in the dark. Many bats use echolocation and sight together.

Sources

  • Bat Conservation International: Blind as a Bat? No Such Thing
  • USGS: Are bats blind?
  • Current Biology: Bats: Vision or echolocation, why not both?
