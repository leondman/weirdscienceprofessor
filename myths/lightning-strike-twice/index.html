Lightning DOES strike twice

The Professor says

Lightning never strikes the same place twice.

Fact Check

It does, and often. Tall, pointed and isolated objects get hit again and again, sometimes more than once in a single storm. Lightning does not avoid places it has already hit.

Sources

US National Weather Service, Live Science (lightning expert Martin Uman, University of Florida)
