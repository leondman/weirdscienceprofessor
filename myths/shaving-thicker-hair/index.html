Myths · 2026-10-12

Shaving doesn't thicken hair. The razor tip tricks your eyes.

The Professor says

Shaving makes your hair grow back thicker.

Fact Check

It doesn't. Shaving cuts each hair straight across at its widest point, so the regrowing stubble has a blunt tip instead of a fine tapered one. That makes it feel coarser and look darker. The hair itself, and the follicle under the skin, are unchanged.

Sources

Mayo Clinic, Scientific American
