Why a night race can still overheat the driver

The Professor says

A night race must be cool enough to keep the driver from overheating.

Fact Check

Night does not remove the heat and humidity. Sweat cools the body when it evaporates; high humidity makes it feel hotter (heat index). Formula 1 describes Singapore as a night race with a hot cockpit, little cooling airflow and drivers working in fireproof overalls and a helmet. Those conditions can still put a heavy heat load on a driver.

Sources

  • National Weather Service: Heat index and sweat evaporation
  • Formula 1: How drivers prepare for Singapore
