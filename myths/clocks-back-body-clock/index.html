Clocks back. Body clock reset?

An extra hour on the clock is not an instant reset for the clocks in your body.

The Professor says

"I've wound myself an extra hour!"

Fact Check

Light and dark help set your daily body rhythms. A master clock in your brain helps coordinate clocks throughout your body. Turning clock hands back does not instantly reset that system.

The UK clocks went back on Sunday 25 October 2026.

Check the sources

  • GOV.UK: When do the clocks change? - the UK's clock-change dates.
  • NIH / NIGMS: Circadian Rhythms - light and dark, the brain's master clock and clocks throughout the body.
