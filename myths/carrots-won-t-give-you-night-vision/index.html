Carrots won't give you night vision.

The Professor says

Carrots give you superhuman night vision.

Fact Check

Carrots contain beta-carotene, which the body can convert into vitamin A. Vitamin A helps normal vision in dim light. Adequate vitamin A supports eye health, but it cannot give night-vision superpowers. Deficiency can cause night blindness; treating a deficiency is different from boosting normal vision.

Sources

  • NHS: Vitamin A
  • Harvard Nutrition Source: Vitamin A
