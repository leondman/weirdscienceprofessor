Sugar does NOT make kids hyper

The Professor says

Sugar makes kids hyper.

Fact Check

It does not. A 1995 JAMA meta-analysis pooled 23 blinded, placebo-controlled studies and found that sugar does not affect children's behaviour or thinking. Parents who expect a sugar rush tend to see one, which is expectation. The authors note a small effect, or an effect in some subsets of children, can't be ruled out.

Sources

  • Wolraich, Wilson and White, JAMA 1995: The Effect of Sugar on Behavior or Cognition in Children (meta-analysis)
