The Professor Got All 5 Wrong

The Professor says

We use only 10% of our brains; shaving thickens hair; goldfish remember for three seconds; lightning never strikes twice; gum stays in the stomach for seven years.

Fact Check

Over a day we use virtually every part of the brain, not just 10%. Shaving does not change hair thickness, colour or growth rate: a blunt tip can feel coarser. Goldfish can learn and retain information far longer than three seconds, including for months. Lightning can strike the same place repeatedly, especially tall isolated objects. Swallowed gum usually passes through relatively intact rather than staying for seven years, though large amounts combined with constipation can rarely cause a blockage in children.
