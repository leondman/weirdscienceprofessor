Cracking your knuckles does NOT cause arthritis

The Professor says

Cracking your knuckles gives you arthritis.

Fact Check

It probably doesn't. Studies comparing habitual knuckle-crackers with non-crackers found no higher rate of hand arthritis. There is a catch: chronic cracking may reduce grip strength, and a couple of injuries have been reported.
